Lead Poisoning Prevention for Childcare Providers

Lead is a licensing and public-health issue California childcare providers should understand from both the education and facility side.

Lead prevention is part of new-provider education

California has added prevention of lead exposure to the preventive health training expectations for new childcare applicants. That means lead is not a side topic—it belongs in the health and safety knowledge base of a provider.

Parent information matters

California Child Care Licensing provides a lead-poisoning information flyer for licensees to provide to parents and families upon enrolling or reenrolling a child. Use the state’s current version and translations rather than keeping an old local copy indefinitely.

Child Care Centers also need to understand water-testing rules

California maintains lead-testing information and guidance related to drinking water in licensed Child Care Centers. Requirements, testing cycles, funding opportunities and implementation guidance can change, so the official CDSS lead page should be your source of truth.
