California Childcare Licensing Checklist

A practical checklist to help you organize the licensing process without getting lost in paperwork.

Use this as an organizer. Your exact requirements depend on facility type, role and current California rules. Always verify items with CCLD.

Before you apply

Choose the right license type. Determine whether you are opening a Family Child Care Home or a Child Care Center. Then complete the corresponding licensing orientation and read the current regulations for that category.

Complete training early

Do not wait until the end of your application to schedule required training. Preventive Health & Safety and Pediatric CPR/First Aid are separate training components, and mandated reporter training is separate again. Completing them early keeps certificates from becoming a bottleneck.

Read the training explainer →

Clear background-check requirements

Identify which adults and staff must complete background checks and follow the current Guardian/Live Scan process. This is an area where following the latest state instructions matters.

Build your document file

Create one physical or digital licensing folder for application forms, facility documents, training certificates, health records, emergency information and any correspondence from your licensing office. Good organization now makes inspections and renewals easier later.

Prepare the facility using the regulations

Do not rely only on someone else’s “daycare setup” checklist. Use the applicable Title 22 regulations, provider videos and your licensing orientation materials to confirm your space, storage, supervision plan, emergency procedures, records and age-specific requirements.

Bookmark ongoing updates

Provider Information Notices are one of the best places to watch for current Child Care Licensing guidance and implementation updates. Check them regularly after you are licensed.
