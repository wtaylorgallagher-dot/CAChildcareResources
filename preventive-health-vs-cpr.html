Preventive Health vs. CPR & First Aid

These courses are connected to California childcare licensing, but they teach different skills and are not interchangeable.

Preventive Health & Safety focuses on prevention

The preventive health course is built around preventing illness and injury in childcare settings and includes current required content such as nutrition and lead poisoning prevention for new applicants. It is not a CPR course.

CPR & First Aid focuses on emergency response

Pediatric CPR and First Aid teach providers how to respond when an emergency has already happened. These skills require practical, hands-on competency and are renewed on a recurring basis.

Why providers mix them up

California often discusses these requirements together as part of childcare health and safety training. That makes it sound like one class, but providers should plan for distinct course components and certificates.

Which should you schedule first?

If you are a new applicant, schedule both early. Preventive Health & Safety can be completed through a live online approved course, while CPR/First Aid involves hands-on skill training. Early scheduling prevents your licensing file from being delayed by class availability.
