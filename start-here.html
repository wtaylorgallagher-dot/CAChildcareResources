New California childcare provider

Start here: your licensing roadmap.

This page gives you a practical order of operations and sends you to the official California source for every major step.

Important: California requirements differ depending on whether you are opening a Family Child Care Home, working in a Child Care Center, or applying for another facility type. Use this roadmap as an organizer—not a replacement for your licensing analyst or official regulations.

1. Decide which childcare path you are taking

Your first question is whether you are opening a licensed Family Child Care Home or working with/opening a Child Care Center. The application, staffing and facility requirements are not identical.

Start with CDSS: How to Become Licensed →

2. Complete the required orientation

Prospective licensees typically begin with a Community Care Licensing orientation for their facility type. The orientation explains the licensing process, application expectations, regulations and next steps.

Child Care Licensing Orientations →

3. Complete required health & safety training

California childcare health and safety training commonly includes Pediatric First Aid, Pediatric CPR and preventive health practices. New applicants may also need required content such as nutrition and prevention of lead exposure as part of the preventive health course.

Preventive Health & Safety: This is separate from CPR/First Aid. Acute Preventive Health and CA Preventive Health & Safety both provide live online CA-EMSA approved Preventive Health & Safety training that includes Nutrition and Lead Poisoning Prevention.

See our training requirement explainer →

4. Complete mandated reporter training

California requires specified licensed childcare providers, applicants, directors and employees to complete mandated reporter training and renew it every two years. Use the state-approved training source and retain your completion certificate.

CDSS training & educational resources →

5. Complete background checks / Live Scan requirements

Applicants and other required adults may need criminal background clearances. California uses Guardian for many background-check processes. Follow the current licensing instructions carefully because who must be cleared depends on the facility and role.

Guardian background check information →

6. Gather your application forms and supporting records

Applications can involve multiple licensing forms, health statements, emergency plans, facility sketches, personnel records, proof of training and other documents. Work directly from the current CCLD forms library rather than an old checklist downloaded from a third party.

California forms library →

7. Prepare your childcare environment

Use Title 22, licensing guidance and provider videos to prepare the facility. Common focus areas include supervision, emergency preparedness, safe sleep where applicable, storage of hazardous items, records, posted documents, sanitation, food safety and age-appropriate equipment.

Child Care Licensing laws & regulations →

8. Stay current after you are licensed

Licensing is not a one-time paperwork event. Bookmark Provider Information Notices (PINs), sign up for CCLD updates, keep renewal-dependent training current and periodically review your records and facility against current requirements.

Current Child Care Provider Information Notices →
