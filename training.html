California childcare training

Know which class does what.

Preventive Health & Safety and Pediatric CPR/First Aid are related licensing requirements—but they are not the same course.

Preventive Health & Safety

This course covers preventive health practices for childcare settings and, for current California applicants, includes required topics such as nutrition and prevention of lead exposure. California EMSA approves preventive health training programs for childcare providers.

Acute Preventive Health and CA Preventive Health & Safety both provide CA-EMSA approved 8-hour Preventive Health & Safety training live online via Zoom.

Pediatric CPR & First Aid

CPR and First Aid are separate from Preventive Health & Safety. California childcare providers should use an EMSA-approved program that meets the applicable childcare training requirements. CPR/First Aid training requires hands-on skills and renewal requirements differ from the one-time preventive health course.

Mandated Reporter Training

Mandated reporter training is another separate requirement for specified childcare licensees, applicants, directors and employees and is renewed every two years.
